About FocuSee

Your Trusted Supplier of Kitchen & Coffee Equipment

Quality of the components, customer service, time reliability. Our company was born more than 10 years ago in order to propose to the international markets professional catering equipment which would distinguish itself for its features. A target on which we remained loyal till today and which allowed us to grow incessantly. We are sure that only in this way our equipment will always be able to keep quality standard high, which make FocuSee remarkable in many countries of the world.

Professional Coffee/Bar/Kitchen Equipment OEM/ODM Supplier

FocuSee is dedicated to offering a wide array of services tailored specifically for the food and beverage industry. It excels in designing, manufacturing, and customizing high-quality equipment essential for coffee shops, bars, and professional kitchens. What sets FocuSee apart is its capacity to deliver customized solutions that span from the initial concept design to the final product, catering to businesses seeking to develop their own brand of professional-grade equipment.

We are professional in the design, development, and manufacturing of premium Italian-style coffee machines, all-in-one grind & brew systems, and coffee grinders. Beyond coffee technology, we also excel in professional kitchen equipment, heating element production, and global import-export trade, offering a vertically integrated solution for partners worldwide.

Our factory and office located in Shunde, Guangdong Province, it covers an area of 5,000 square meters of whole assembling production line, stainless steel cutting, bending, welding, polishing process. Complete processing machinery is under our own control helping us create well finishing product to reach made in Europe quality level.

Frequently Asked Questions

What materials are FocuSee kitchen utensils made of?
FocuSee kitchen equipment is made of high-quality, food-grade stainless steel including SUS430 and SUS420 grades. We use these materials for their durability, rust resistance, and easy-to-clean properties. Our coffee machines feature ABS bodies suitable for various color coatings, aluminum alloy portafilters, and premium heating elements.
Do you offer OEM/ODM services?
Yes, we specialize in OEM/ODM services. We can customize products from initial concept design to final production, including custom branding, packaging, and specifications. Our team has decades of experience in design and marketing to ensure your custom products are both functional and marketable.
What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
MOQ varies by product type. For coffee machines, we offer flexible minimums suitable for both boutique brands and large-scale retailers. Please contact our sales team for specific MOQ information for your desired products.
What certifications do your products have?
Our products hold over 35 international certifications. Our equipment meets rigorous quality standards for markets worldwide. Specific certifications can be provided upon request for your target market.
How long does production and shipping take?
Production lead times depend on order quantity and customization requirements. Our vertically integrated manufacturing process with in-house component production helps ensure shorter lead times. Our trade division handles global logistics for seamless delivery to your market.
Where is your factory located?
Our factory and office are located in Shunde, Guangdong Province, China. The facility covers 5,000 square meters with complete assembling production lines including stainless steel cutting, bending, welding, and polishing processes.
Can you provide product samples?
Yes, we can provide product samples for evaluation before placing bulk orders. Sample fees and shipping charges may apply. Please contact our team to discuss sample arrangements for your specific requirements.
